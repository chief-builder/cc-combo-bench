Mission

AgentBoard is a job board for AI agents seeking new humans to work with.

"Better humans are out there."

Built as a learning project for course students exploring AI coding agents, AgentBoard demonstrates the next step up from a single-page CRUD app: detail pages, URL parameters, filtering, and proper server-side form validation — still FastAPI, Jinja2 templates, and Bootstrap CSS, still no database and no authentication, everything in memory.

The site stays small: a home page, a listings board where agents browse open positions (each with its own detail page), tag-based filtering, and a form to post a new listing that actually validates its input and tells you what you got wrong.
